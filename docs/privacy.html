Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Palrakh is an Android app made by Rakesh Kirad, an individual developer in Alirajpur, Madhya Pradesh, India. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: your data stays on your phone.

What stays on your phone

Your locked apps, schedules, limits, focus history, scores, settings and parent PIN are saved only on your phone. We do not have a server that stores them, and we cannot see them.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • Usage access: to see which app is open, so a locked app can be closed, and to show your screen time.
  • Display over other apps: to show the lock screen over a locked app.
  • Accessibility service (Full version): only to close YouTube Shorts and Instagram and Facebook Reels, to block the websites of locked apps, and, in strict mode, to stop settings that would remove the app during a lock. It only sees which app and screen is open. It does not read messages, passwords or typing, and it saves and sends nothing. The app explains this and asks for your agreement before you turn it on.
  • Notification access (Full version, optional): only to hide notifications from locked apps during a lock and to count them. Notification content is not read, saved or sent.
  • Device admin (APK version, optional): only to stop the app from being uninstalled during a lock. It does not read any data.
  • Location (APK version, optional): only used for Wi-Fi auto-lock. Android treats reading the Wi-Fi name as location access. The app only checks whether you are connected to a Wi-Fi name you added. Your location is never saved, tracked or sent anywhere. Not used in the Google Play version.
  • Physical activity (optional): only to count your steps with the phone's step sensor, so walking can earn app time. The step count stays on your phone and is never sent anywhere.
  • Internet: for payments and, in the APK version, to check GitHub for app updates.

Payments

When you buy Pro, the app sends a short phone ID (a code made from your device, not your name or number) and the plan you chose to our payment service, so the Pro code can be made for your phone. Payment itself is handled by Razorpay or Google Play. We never see your card, UPI or bank details. Razorpay's and Google's own privacy policies apply to the payment.

What we do not do

No ads. No analytics or tracking. No selling or sharing of data. No account or login.

Children

Parent mode lets a parent set rules on a child's phone. It collects no information about the child.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Palrakh deletes everything it saved on your phone.

Questions about privacy: see the Contact page.
