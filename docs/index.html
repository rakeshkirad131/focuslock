What Palrakh does

Focus lock

Pick apps and a time. They stay closed until the time is up.

Shorts and Reels block

YouTube Shorts and Instagram and Facebook Reels close as soon as they open. The rest of the app still works.

Website block

A locked app's website is blocked in Chrome and other browsers too.

Daily schedules

Sleep, study or family time: apps lock on their own every day.

Daily limits

Give each app a daily limit. When it runs out, the app waits until midnight.

Parent mode

For a child's phone. Rules change only with the parent's PIN.

Pricing
