Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Cancellation

Pro is sold as one-time passes (3, 30 or 365 days). There is no subscription and nothing renews, so there is nothing to cancel.

Refunds

You can ask for a full refund within 7 days of payment if:

  • Pro did not turn on after a successful payment and we could not fix it, or
  • you were charged twice for the same purchase.

Write to us from the Contact page with the payment ID or screenshot and your phone ID (shown in the app under Pro). Approved refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days.

Purchases made through Google Play follow Google Play's refund process.
